Spiral Stage

Overlay on your screen

Shows the layers on top of any app (Word, YouTube, a video player). Clicks pass through to the app underneath.

Hotkeys that work in any app:
Ctrl+Alt+O overlay on or off
Ctrl+Alt+P show or hide this panel
Ctrl+Alt+H hide everything right now
Ctrl+Alt+Space pause or resume

Subliminal text

Flashes are held to at most 2.5 per second to reduce seizure risk.

Tones and binaural beats

Use headphones for binaural beats. Start with the volume low. Hide all (Ctrl+Alt+H) and pause also silence the tone.

Background sound

Generated live on your PC, so it works offline.

    Before you start

    Spirals and flashing text can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy. Don't use this if you or anyone in your family has a history of seizures.

    Stop right away if you feel dizzy, nauseous or unwell. Don't use it while driving or operating machinery.
